About Me

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Associate Professor Feng Liu. I received my B.Eng. degree from Tsinghua University in 2024.

My research focuses on reliable and scalable stability analysis for converter-dominated power systems. In particular, I am interested in understanding when reduced-order models remain trustworthy across electromagnetic and electromechanical time scales, and in developing compositional or distributed stability certificates that connect local device/network information to system-level behavior.

My current research can be summarized by two questions: when can simplified models be trusted? and how can stability be certified locally but guaranteed globally?

Research Interests

Multi-timescale Modeling & Robust Stability. EMT-Phasor/RMS interaction, model validity, structured uncertainty, structured singular value (mu), robust stability, and terminal frequency-response based uncertainty identification.
Compositional & Distributed Stability. Passivity/dissipativity, local-to-global stability certificates, distributed stability margins, heterogeneous converter dynamics, scalable robust analysis, and stability-oriented grid codes.
